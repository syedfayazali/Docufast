How it works

From the customer's phone to your printer in four steps

  1. Customer scans your QR

    Your shop has its own link and QR code, so every order lands in your queue.

  2. Uploads and pays

    They choose copies, colour and sides, see the price, and pay by UPI.

  3. It prints by itself

    A small app on your shop PC picks up the paid job and sends it to your printer.

  4. You hand it over

    The order appears on your live board with a short code the customer shows you.

Questions

Before you start

What do I need in my shop?

A Windows PC connected to your printer, an internet connection, and a phone that accepts UPI. You install the DocuFast app on the PC once.

Which printers work?

The app prints through Windows, so printers that print normally from your PC are supported. We check your printer with you during setup.

What happens to my customers' documents?

Customers give consent before uploading. Files are stored privately, opened only for printing, and deleted after printing, with a short reprint window you can set per shop.

Do customers need to install anything?

No. They scan your QR code and use their phone's browser. The page works in English, Telugu and Hindi.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Start with the free trial and continue only if it works for your shop.

Who helps me set up?

We do. After you send the form we call you, create your shop, and walk you through installing the app and printing a test order.
