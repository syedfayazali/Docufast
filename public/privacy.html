Privacy Policy

DocuFast India, Mahabubnagar, Telangana · Last updated: 18 November 2026

What we collect

Customers: the files you upload to print, your print choices (pages, copies, colour), and payment details handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

Shops: shop name, owner name, phone number, email address, and information about the computer running the DocuFast app (a computer ID and name) so each subscription is used on the computer it was bought for.

How we use it

Your files are used only to print your order at the shop you chose. We do not read them, sell them, or use them for advertising. Phone numbers and emails are used to run your account, send sign-in codes and contact you about your order or subscription.

How long we keep files

Files are stored privately and are deleted automatically soon after printing (the shop sets how soon), and uploads that are never paid for are removed within hours. Order records (without the file) are kept for accounting.

Who can see your data

Only the shop that prints your order can open your file, through a private, time-limited link. Our service providers process data for us: Vercel (hosting and file storage), Supabase (database), Razorpay (payments) and Brevo (email).

Sensitive documents

If you print an ID document such as an Aadhaar card, take it from the printer yourself and do not leave it at the counter. File names are masked where possible, but you are responsible for what you upload.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your data or correct it by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the contact details on software.docufastindia.com.
